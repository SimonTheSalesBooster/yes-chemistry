# The science behind the Yes Chemistry (and its limits)

The behaviour findings below are solid. "Chemical X at step Y" is shorthand for a buying moment, not a lab measurement.

| Claim | Source | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| People with damaged emotional brain regions keep their logic but struggle to decide anything | Antonio Damasio, *Descartes' Error* (1994) | No feeling, no decision |
| Dopamine signals expected reward and fires harder when a reward beats expectations; it drops below baseline when an expected reward doesn't come | Wolfram Schultz (1997 onward) | Wanting, and why a big promise followed by a thin call backfires |
| Curiosity activates reward circuits and improves memory | Gruber, Gelman & Ranganath, *Neuron* (2014) | The rabbit hole (Step 2) |
| Talking about yourself activates the same reward regions as food and money | Tamir & Mitchell, *PNAS* (2012) | Being seen (Step 1) |
| Mirroring increases liking and smooths interaction | Chartrand & Bargh (1999) | Visualize (Step 1) |
| Deep conversations are enjoyed far more than people predict, and connect more than small talk | Kardas, Kumar & Epley (2022) | Skip the bookshelf line |
| Losses weigh about twice as much as equal gains | Kahneman & Tversky, prospect theory (1979) | Cost of Inaction (Step 4) |
| A price that feels too high activates the insula (linked to pain); when that outweighs the reward signal, people don't buy | Knutson et al., *Neuron* (2007) | Price after pain (Step 6) |
| Rowers training together showed a higher pain threshold (an endorphin marker) than training alone | Cohen et al., *Biology Letters* (2009) | Shared effort after the yes |
| Social laughter raises pain threshold, consistent with endorphin release | Dunbar et al. (2012) | Laughter builds the bond |

## Honest notes
1. **Oxytocin is the shakiest.** The famous "oxytocin nasal spray increases trust" result (Kosfeld et al., 2005) did not replicate well (see Nave, Camerer & McCullough, 2015). Read it as "belonging", not "a spray that makes people trust you".
2. **Dopamine is wanting, not happiness.** It gets people leaning forward. It doesn't keep them.
3. **Cortisol is a stress hormone.** In a sales call it stands for urgency the buyer feels about their own real cost. Manufactured pressure is manipulation and breaks trust.
4. **It's a model.** Many chemicals act at once, all the time. The order of the buying moments is what you control.

## About the name
"DOSE" (dopamine, oxytocin, serotonin, endorphins) is a common wellness acronym and a registered brand in other markets. The Yes Chemistry adds cortisol on purpose and puts all five in the order a buying decision needs.
