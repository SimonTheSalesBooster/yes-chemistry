**0/14, A PITCH, NOT A CONVERSATION.** Red flags: PRICE BEFORE PAIN, PROPOSAL WITHOUT A DATE, NO RABBIT HOLE.

## 3 fixes
1. **Replace the screen share (line 1)** with: "Before I show you anything.. what's the one thing in sales that frustrates you most right now, and what have you already tried?" Then stay there.
2. **Before any number (line 5)** ask: "And if nothing changes for the next six months, what does that cost you?" Wait. Let the buyer say it.
3. **When they ask for a proposal (line 6)** answer: "Happy to. Which starting date should it say?" No date, no proposal yet.
